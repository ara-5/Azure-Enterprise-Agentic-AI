# Incident Response Runbook (Sample)

## Severity Definitions and SLAs
Severity 1 (complete outage or data loss risk): must be acknowledged within 15 minutes of alert and
have an initial mitigation attempt within 1 hour. The on-call engineer pages the incident commander
immediately and opens a live bridge.

Severity 2 (major functionality degraded): acknowledged within 30 minutes, initial mitigation within
4 hours.

Severity 3 (minor, non-customer-facing): acknowledged within 1 business day.

## Postmortem Process
Every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident requires a blameless postmortem published within 5 business
days, including a timeline, root cause, and follow-up action items with owners and due dates.
