# HR Handbook (Sample)

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements
require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly. Employees must be available during core hours
(10am-3pm local time) regardless of work location.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued monthly starting from
their hire date. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year. PTO requests
should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Onboarding
New hires complete a 2-week onboarding program covering company systems access, security training,
and team introductions. IT provisions laptop and accounts within the first 3 business days.
