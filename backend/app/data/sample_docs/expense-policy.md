# Expense Policy (Sample)

## Approval Thresholds
Expense reports up to $1,000 require manager approval only. Reports between $1,000 and $5,000
require manager and finance business partner approval. Expense reports over $5,000 require
VP-level approval in addition to the manager's approval, and must include an itemized business
justification.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit. Receipts are required
for any single expense over $25.
